Privacy Policy

1. Data Used on Your Device

Orbit Steps, published by Hari Rajagopalan, reads your daily and historical steps, flights climbed, exercise minutes, and walking speed from Apple Health only with permission. Distance and calories are estimated from steps; active time may also be estimated when exercise minutes are unavailable. Your goal, preferences, favorites, session timing, and achievements are saved locally. No account is required.

2. Optional Outdoor Routes

Route recording is off unless you enable it. With location permission and Precise Location, Orbit records GPS points during an active interval walk, including while the screen is locked. Recording pauses when the session is paused and stops when it ends. Walks work without location access. Turning recording off affects future walks; it does not erase saved routes.

3. Storage & Retention

Orbit does not operate a server for your fitness data or sync it to a developer cloud account. Step caches, saved walks, GPS routes, and preferences containing activity data are stored on this device and marked as excluded from device backups. This also applies to the data shared locally with the Orbit widget. Saved history has no automatic 30-day expiration.

Saved walks and routes may be lost if you delete the app, lose this device, or restore onto another device. Apple Health maintains its own data and sync settings separately. This update cannot remove copies already included in older backups.

4. How Your Data Is Used

Local data powers your activity display, goal progress, streaks, history, trends, recaps, interval coaching, and optional route maps. On supported iOS versions, Orbit requests permission to run a HealthKit workout session to keep interval timing and coaching active in the background. Completed interval records are saved in Orbit; Orbit does not modify your step samples or save completed workouts to Apple Health.

5. Sharing & Apple Services

Orbit includes no advertising, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking, and does not sell your health or location data.

Apple Maps supplies map imagery when you view a route; this requires a request for the displayed map area to Apple. GPS route lines are drawn on your device. Apple services follow Apple’s privacy policies.

Step exports and session or recap images leave the app only when you choose to save or share them through the system share sheet. Session sharing includes a route map only if you turn on Include route map; this reveals the recorded location to the recipient. The service or recipient you choose then handles that copy. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to send through our email provider. Please avoid including sensitive health or route details unless needed for your request.

6. Your Choices & Deletion

You can manage Orbit’s Health permissions in the Health app, and location permissions in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Orbit. Disabling permission stops new access but does not erase already saved local records.

Export Step History in Settings shares your available daily step totals; it is not a backup of sessions or GPS routes. Delete App in iPhone Storage removes Orbit’s local records; Offload App keeps its documents and data. Deleting Orbit does not delete source records in Apple Health, files you exported, recipients’ copies, or old backups. Manage those separately in the relevant Apple or other service.

We cannot access or recover your on-device records. Contact us about privacy questions or rights concerning information you sent to support; applicable rights depend on your location.

7. Coaching, Notifications & Widgets

Speech and chimes use on-device audio; Orbit does not record your microphone. Optional local alerts, widgets, and Live Activities may show activity or session information on the Lock Screen or a paired Apple Watch. Control notification previews, widgets, and Live Activities in iOS settings. No developer push-notification server is used.

8. Children & Support

Orbit is a general wellness app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not operate accounts or collect children’s fitness records on a server. If you believe a child sent personal information to support, contact rajhari@gmail.com so we can address the request. Support correspondence is used to respond and retained only as needed for support or applicable obligations.

9. Policy Updates

This policy describes version 2.1. Material changes will be reflected in the policy and its effective date. Where new access requires permission, Orbit will request it through the relevant system controls.

Orbit Steps is intended for general wellness, not the diagnosis, treatment, monitoring, or prevention of a medical condition.

2. Not Medical Advice

All step counts, distance data, calorie estimates, and metrics are for informational and motivational purposes only. They do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Never delay seeking professional medical advice because of information in this app.

3. Consult a Healthcare Professional

Consult a qualified physician before starting any new exercise program, especially if you:

• Have or suspect a heart condition
• Have diabetes, hypertension, or chronic illness
• Are pregnant or recently postpartum
• Are recovering from surgery or injury
• Experience pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath

4. Accuracy Limitations

Steps and available activity metrics come from Apple Health. Distance and calories are calculated estimates from steps, not direct measurements; active time may be estimated when exercise minutes are unavailable. GPS routes and coaching cues can be incomplete or delayed. Do not use these features for clinical decisions or emergency navigation.

5. Physical Activity Risk

Choose a pace and duration that feel comfortable and pay attention to your surroundings. Open-ended walks do not stop automatically; pause or finish whenever you need to. Do not pursue a goal or streak at the expense of your well-being.
