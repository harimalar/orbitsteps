## Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: February 18, 2026**

### 1. Overview
Orbit Steps ("the App") is designed with your privacy as the top priority. The App integrates with Apple's HealthKit to display your step count and walking distance. We do not collect, store, or share your personal health data on our servers.

### 2. Data Collection and Usage
- **HealthKit Data:** The App requests permission to read step count and distance data from Apple Health. This data is displayed only within the App and its widgets.
- **Local Processing:** All data processing happens locally on your device.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use third-party analytics services that track your behavior or personal information.

### 3. Data Storage
The App does not maintain its own database of your health records. Your data resides in the Apple Health app on your iPhone and is backed up to iCloud according to your personal settings.

### 4. Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to outside parties. Your health data is not shared with any advertising networks or data brokers.

### 5. Your Consent
By using our App, you consent to this privacy policy. You can revoke the App's access to HealthKit at any time via your iPhone Settings -> Health -> Data Access & Devices -> Orbit Steps.

### 6. Contact Us
If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, you may email us at **rajhari@gmail.com**.
